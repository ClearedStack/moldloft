Sample performance period allowance

Counts calendar days between two ISO-8601 dates and multiplies inclusive performance days by a made-up daily allowance. Every rule and number here is invented to exercise date inputs in the calculator template.

What this tool does not cover

  • Every rule, date range and rate here is fictional. Do not use the result for a real travel voucher, period of performance or contract claim.
  • Counts calendar days on the proleptic Gregorian calendar and does not adjust for federal holidays or partial travel days.

How the result is worked out

StepWhat it isHow it is worked outResultSource
1 Elapsed calendar days (days) Calendar days from Period start date to Period end date, rounded to a whole number. - [1]
2 Elapsed days plus start day (days) Elapsed calendar days + Inclusive endpoint day, rounded to a whole number. - [1]
3 Billable calendar days (days) The largest of: Elapsed days plus start day; Minimum billable days, rounded to a whole number. - [1]
4 Total period allowance Billable calendar days × Daily allowance rate, rounded to 2 decimal places. - [2]

Every step uses exact decimal arithmetic. Where a step is rounded, a half is rounded away from zero: 0.125 to 2 decimal places is 0.13.
